Things To Do

Everything to see and do, from resort amenities to North Shore adventures. Tap any tile for details.

We've organized this by where it is — what's included right here at Kuilima, what's at Turtle Bay Resort (open to everyone, most things booked and paid separately), and what's worth the drive beyond the resort. Look for ⭐ on our favorites.

At Kuilima Estates West Included for guests
🏊Pools

Several community pools throughout Kuilima West, including one just a short walk away. Access details are in your welcome info.

🎾Tennis & Pickleball

Two courts on property, free for guests to use — no reservation required. Pickleball paddles are provided in the unit.

At Turtle Bay Resort Open to all · most activities paid

The resort grounds, beach, and dining are free to enjoy for everyone. The resort pool and hot tub require a day pass, and activities like golf, horseback riding, and surf lessons are booked and paid separately — no day pass or resort stay required.

🌊Resort Grounds & Pool Bar ⭐

Free to enjoy — walk the grounds or relax on the edge of Kuilima Cove. Grab a bite and a drink at the Sunset Pool Bar (see Food & Drink for hours and menu).

🍔Lei Lei's Bar & Grill

A casual, open-air spot at the golf clubhouse, run by the resort — the closest restaurant to Kuilima Estates West. Great for breakfast, lunch, or dinner (see Food & Drink for hours).

🏝️Kuilima Cove ⭐

A protected, sandy cove right at the resort with calm water — great for swimming and relaxing. It's a public beach, free and open to everyone.

🚶Coastal Sunrise Walk ⭐

Start your day with a walk along the coastal paths near Turtle Bay Resort — free, scenic, and best in the early morning light.

🏖️Resort Pool & Hot Tub Day pass

Four resort pools, waterslides, and a hot tub — accessible with a day pass (from around $120) purchased through the resort.

⛳Golf

Two championship courses — the Arnold Palmer Course and the George Fazio Course (course availability can vary, so check current status). Book tee times directly through Turtle Bay Golf.

🐎Horseback Riding ⭐

Trail rides along oceanfront paths and through ironwood forests. Book through The Stables at Turtle Bay.

🐴Keiki Pony Rides & Experiences

Kid-focused experiences at The Stables, with options for ages 7 and up as well as younger keiki. Learn more and book at Turtle Bay Experiences.

🏄Surf Lessons ⭐

Learn to surf right at the resort with the Jamie O'Brien Surf Experience, Turtle Bay's exclusive surf partner.

💆Spa

Nalu Spa, right at the resort. Open 8am–6pm daily. Call 808.447.6868 to book your treatment.

🚁Helicopter Tours

Tours depart right from the resort's helipad at Kuilima Point. Inquire with the concierge, or book directly with Blue Hawaiian Helicopters.

🌺Hula, Lei & Ukulele

Occasional lessons hosted at the resort, typically an added cost. Contact the resort directly for current pricing and availability.

💍Weddings & Special Events

A chapel near Kuilima Point can be booked for weddings and other special occasions, along with other oceanfront venues at the resort. Out-of-town guests can stay right on-site at the resort, making it easy to host a full wedding weekend — it's also a beautiful setting for a vow renewal. Learn more.

Beyond The Resort

Just a short drive away, but worth it for those who want to see and do more of what the North Shore has to offer.

🌅Sunset Beach ⭐

One of the North Shore's most famous beaches, and one of our favorite spots to watch the sun go down.

🌿Waimea Valley ⭐

Waterfall, botanical gardens, and Hawaiian cultural sites along an easy paved path. Plan your visit.

🐢Shark's Cove & Laniakea ⭐

Snorkel the tide pools at Shark's Cove (summer months) and visit the sleeping sea turtles at Laniakea Beach. Please maintain at least 10 feet of distance from the turtles — it's the law, and it keeps them safe and undisturbed.

🛍️Haleiwa Town ⭐

The historic surf town, a 20-minute drive down the road — boutiques, surf shops, and local restaurants.

🌴Zipline Adventure

O'ahu's longest zipline over a working farm at CLIMB Works Keana Farms in Kahuku.

🦈Shark Cage Diving

View sharks safely from a protective cage just offshore. Book through Hawaii Shark Encounters.

🤿Cage-Free Shark Diving

Swim alongside sharks with no cage, guided by marine biologists, with One Ocean Diving.

🐋Whale & Dolphin Watching

Seasonal humpback whale watching (winter) departs from Haleiwa Harbor aboard the Ho'o Nanea catamaran with North Shore Catamaran Charters.

🎣Deep-Sea Fishing

Charters targeting marlin, mahi-mahi, and ahi depart from Haleiwa Harbor with Go Fishing Hawaii.

🪂Skydiving

Tandem skydiving over the North Shore, departing from Dillingham Airfield in Mokule'ia. Two operators to choose from: Skydive Hawaii and Pacific Skydiving Center.

At the resort

Sunset Pool Bar

Right at the pool, with front-row views of the surf break and ocean beyond. Order a Mai Tai, grab some food, and watch surfers from one of the best seats on the North Shore. Open to non-resort guests — live music Thursday & Sunday evenings. Open 10am–8pm, food service 11am–7pm. View menu.

At the resort · Golf clubhouse

Lei Lei's Bar & Grill

Casual, open-air dining run by the resort at the golf clubhouse — the closest restaurant to Kuilima Estates West. Fresh fish, burgers, and poke, with views of the course and the North Shore. Try the Lei Lei's Lemonade or their signature Arnold Palmer. Breakfast 7–10:30am, lunch 10:30am–4pm, dinner 4–9pm.

At the resort

Beach House by Roy Yamaguchi

Right on the sand at Kuilima Cove — premium beachfront dining and custom cocktails with your toes practically in the water. Open 11:30am–2:30pm and 4:30pm–9pm. Our favorite after-dinner drink: the "Beautiful" — Courvoisier & Grand Marnier, served up in a snifter. View the menu.

🐾 Dog-friendly
Near Turtle Bay · Kahuku

Kahuku Farms

A true farm-to-table experience just minutes from the resort — winner of Newsweek's 2025 Best Farm-to-Table Restaurant in the US. Run by a fourth-generation farming family. Try the acai bowl (made with their own Hawaiian-grown acai), farm pizza, veggie panini, or smoothies. The wagon farm tour is a great add-on. Open Thu–Sun, 11am–4pm. Plan your visit.

Near Turtle Bay · Kahuku Sugar Mill ⭐

Shrimp Trucks & Food Trucks at Kahuku

The Kahuku Sugar Mill is a lively cluster of food trucks, restaurants, and shops on Kamehameha Highway. Two of our favorites for classic garlic shrimp: Big Wave Shrimp Truck and Giovanni's. Seven Brothers Burgers is the anchor — known for massive island-style burgers, garlic shrimp, and coconut macadamia shrimp. Also here: Fumi's for more garlic shrimp, Ry's Poke Shack for fresh ahi, Surf N Salsa for Mexican, and Ono Yo for frozen yogurt. Plan for a wander and pick what looks good.

Pupukea · Near Shark's Cove

Pupukea Food Trucks

Pupukea Grill — right across from Shark's Cove — is a North Shore institution. Fresh poke bowls, sushi, kalua pork bowls, wraps, smoothie bowls, and vegan options. The spicy tuna poke bowl is legendary. Da Bald Guy nearby does outstanding plate lunches — kalbi beef and chopped rib eye are the move. Open Tue–Sun. Pupukea Grill menu.

Haleiwa Town

Haleiwa Eats

The historic surf town is packed with great spots. Uncle Bo's Pupu Bar & Grill for local-style seafood and pupus. Waialua Bakery & Juice Bar for sandwiches, house-baked breads, and smoothies. Matsumoto Shave Ice for the classic Hawaiian treat. Surf N Sea for browsing while you snack.

One-Day Itineraries

Three ways to spend a day on O'ahu, depending on how far you want to roam from Turtle Bay.

The Perfect One-Day North Shore Adventure
Full dayCustomize at 1:30pm
+
  • 7:00 AMSunrise walk along the Turtle Bay coastline. Grab coffee at Ho'olana Cafe at Turtle Bay.
  • 8:00 AMBreakfast at Sunrise Shack or Ted's Bakery.
  • 9:30 AMExplore Waimea Valley — waterfall and botanical gardens.
  • 11:00 AMRelax on the sand at Waimea Bay.
  • 12:00 PMLunch from a food truck at Shark's Cove or in Haleiwa — shrimp trucks like Giovanni's, or poke trucks like Pupukea Grill, are both great options.
  • 1:30 PMChoose your adventure: snorkel at Shark's Cove, a surf lesson, horseback riding, or ziplining. Our favorite in summer is snorkeling at Shark's Cove.
  • 4:00 PMBrowse Haleiwa Town's surf shops and boutiques, grab shave ice. On the drive back, stop at Laniakea Beach for the sleeping sea turtles and Ehukai Beach Park for the famous Pipeline surf break.
  • SunsetA drink by the pool back at Turtle Bay — the full bar there has great views of the ocean and surfers, plus food options if you're hungry.
  • DinnerBeach House by Roy Yamaguchi, right on the beach at the resort and dog-friendly.
East Side Adventure
~3 hrs total drive timeDepart 8:00 AM
+
  • 8:00 AMDepart Turtle Bay Resort. Grab a coffee and enjoy the scenic drive down O'ahu's Windward Coast.
  • 8:45 AMThe Crouching Lion — stop for coffee at their on-site Hala Tree Cafe, open 7am daily. The main restaurant opens for lunch at 11am. (30–45 min)
  • 9:45 AMKualoa Regional Park — a short beach walk with one of O'ahu's most iconic views, Mokoli'i (Chinaman's Hat). (20–30 min)
  • 10:30 AMByodo-In Temple — located within the Valley of the Temples memorial park, explore the grounds, ring the sacred bell, feed the koi, and enjoy the gardens. There is an entrance fee. (~45 min)
  • 11:15 AMHo'omaluhia Botanical Garden — walking paths and mountain views through one of Hawaii's most beautiful gardens. (~45 min)
  • 12:15 PMLunch in Kailua — Kalapawai Market (next to Kailua Beach) or Kalapawai Cafe & Deli (in Kailua town), plus Maui Brewing Co. and Buzz's Original Steak House are all great options.
  • OptionalBrowse Kailua town's cute boutiques — a few of our favorites are Olive Boutique, Red Bamboo, Twin Islands, and Classic Waves.
  • 1:30 PMKailua Beach Park — swim, walk the shore, or just relax on one of Hawaii's most beautiful beaches. (1–2 hrs)
  • OptionalSea Life Park — see turtles and dolphins up close. Open 10am–4pm daily except Tuesdays; tickets are recommended online in advance, and there is a parking fee.
  • 5:30 PMLeave Kailua and enjoy the scenic drive back along the Windward Coast.
  • ~7:00 PMArrive back at Turtle Bay. Finish the day with dinner at the resort or sunset from Kuilima Point.

Insider tips: bring swimsuits and towels for Kailua Beach, wear comfortable shoes for the temple and garden, pack water, sunscreen, and a camera, and start early to keep a relaxed pace and avoid traffic. The drive back to Turtle Bay takes about 1.25 hours, whether you return through the island on the H1 or along the coast.

Waikiki & Honolulu Day
Full dayDiamond Head reservation required
+
  • 7:00 AMHike Diamond Head before it gets hot and crowded — a reservation is required in advance for entry and parking. (60–90 min)
  • 8:30 AMBreakfast nearby before heading into town.
  • 10:00 AMKaka'ako — explore the neighborhood's murals, boutiques, and shops.
  • 11:30 AMLunch at Hana Koa Brewing Co. in Kaka'ako.
  • 1:00 PMVisit a museum — the Honolulu Museum of Art or the Bishop Museum are both excellent choices.
  • 3:00 PMHead to Waikiki for a surf lesson or to relax on the beach.
  • 5:00 PMGrab a drink at Duke's Waikiki, or book an afternoon catamaran sail for ocean views. Our recommendation: a drink at Hau Tree Lanai in the Kaimana Beach Hotel.
  • 7:00 PMDinner in Waikiki, or head to Chinatown — Fête is our recommendation.
  • ~8:30 PMDrive back to Turtle Bay — about 1.5 hours, maybe a little less.

Insider tips: book your Diamond Head reservation online well ahead of time — time slots fill up fast — and start early to make the most of the day before Honolulu traffic picks up.

Prefer an ocean-focused day instead? Swap in Hanauma Bay for snorkeling, Sea Life Park, and the Makapu'u Lighthouse trail.

Good to know

Guest FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from guests at Kuilima Estates West.

🏊Do I have access to Turtle Bay Resort amenities and the pool?
+

Anyone can walk around the resort, visit the beach, and order food and drinks. Resort amenities like the pool, hot tub, and fitness center are reserved for resort guests — but day passes (from around $120), spa passes (from around $50), and cabana rentals are available through the resort if you'd like access. Kuilima Estates West has its own pool and tennis courts for guests to enjoy.

🏖️What items are available in the unit?
+

We stock the condo with everything you need for beach days and island life: beach chairs, umbrellas, beach towels, pool floats, and a pack n' play. Just rinse off the sand and return everything neatly before checkout so the next guest has it too.

🚶How far is the beach, and can we walk there?
+

Yes, the beach is a short walk away, right next to the resort. If you'd rather not walk, there's also public parking available next to the beach access.

🚗Do we need a rental car?
+

We recommend a rental car due to the remote nature of the North Shore and the abundance of restaurants and activities that require at least a short drive.

🐾Are pets allowed at Kuilima Estates West?
+

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed at Kuilima Estates West per the condo association's rules.
